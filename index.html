Fizzy Baking Soda Volcano

Easy • 10 mins

Ingredients Needed:

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Place your plastic cup on a tray or in the sink.
  2. Add 2 tablespoons of baking soda into the cup.
  3. Mix a few drops of red food coloring into the vinegar.
  4. Pour the vinegar in and watch the volcano erupt! 💥
🌈

Rainbow Walking Water

Medium • 30 mins

Ingredients Needed:

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Fill glasses with water and add primary food colors.
  2. Fold paper towels into strips.
  3. Place towel strips connecting the glasses together.
  4. Watch water travel up the towels to mix new secondary colors!
